## Master of Computational Science

Laurentian University, Canada · 2021 – 2023

Mihir's thesis focused on machine learning for geological discovery. He delivered an oral presentation at AICMSE 2023 at the Harvard Faculty Club and co-authored a 2023 publication in Ore Geology Reviews.

## Bachelor of Computer Engineering

Gujarat Technological University, India · 2013 – 2017

## Publication and Conference Presentation

Mihir co-authored “A study of faults in the Superior province of Ontario and Quebec using random forest ML: Spatial relationship to gold mines,” published in Ore Geology Reviews in 2023 (DOI: 10.1016/j.oregeorev.2023.105403). He also presented “Machine Learning for Predictive Modeling of Mineral Deposits” at AICMSE 2023.
